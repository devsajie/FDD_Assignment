ACADEMIC ALLIANCE

The Origin of APUHACKERX2026 // Bridging Kathmandu & Kuala Lumpur

APUHACKERX2026 emerged from a shared ambition: to build a rigorous, cross-border incubator where student software engineers, hardware tinkerers, and designers from Lord Buddha Education Foundation (LBEF) and Asia Pacific University of Technology & Innovation (APU) tackle genuine regional problems together.

THE MISSION

Why We Hack Together

University education provides solid foundations in computational theory, algorithm design, and software engineering principles. However, real-world problems do not come packaged in chapter exercises.

Kathmandu faces unique infrastructural dynamics—hilly terrain requiring mesh communications, municipal water and air sensing, financial digitization of cash-dominant local markets, and linguistic representation for Nepali languages in artificial intelligence.

By connecting students from APU's technology campus in Malaysia with LBEF's engineering hub in Maitidevi, APUHACKERX2026 sparks a technical cross-pollination that pushes teams beyond conventional hackathon prototypes into production-grade systems.

FOUR PILLARS

Core Principles

PILLAR 01 // ACCESSIBILITY

Zero Pay-to-Play

APUHACKERX2026 is 100% free for all students. Free accommodation on campus, unlimited caffeine, meals, cloud credits, and hardware boards.

PILLAR 02 // TECHNICAL GRIT

Working Code Over PowerPoints

No purely theoretical pitch concepts. Teams must demo functioning repositories, API responses, or physical breadboards on stage.

PILLAR 03 // STUDENT OWNERSHIP

100% Intellectual Property Retention

Participants maintain full ownership of all code, designs, and intellectual property produced during the 48 hours.
