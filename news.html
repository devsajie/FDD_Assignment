PRESS RELEASE PUBLISHED OCT 01, 2026 · 4 MIN READ

APUHACKERX2026 Opens Registration for 50 University Teams Across Nepal & Malaysia

The joint organizing committee of Lord Buddha Education Foundation and Asia Pacific University today officially initiated candidate intake for the upcoming 48-hour hackathon scheduled for November 30 – December 2 in Kathmandu (Registration open till November 20). Selected cohorts will receive free on-campus residency, high-bandwidth compute instances, and direct mentoring from senior regional software architects.
