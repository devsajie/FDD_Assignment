ORIGIN & PURPOSE

An Academic Collision of Talent & Purpose

APUHACKERX2026 is not a corporate showcase or an artificial networking summit. It is an intensive, student-led technical marathon designed to bridge academic theory with real engineering grit.

Established through the collaborative partnership between Lord Buddha Education Foundation (LBEF) in Kathmandu and Asia Pacific University of Technology & Innovation (APU) in Malaysia, APUHACKERX2026 challenges undergraduate innovators to solve problems facing high-density cities and mountainous ecosystems.

Participants are equipped with high-speed campus networks, 24-hour cafeteria meals, dedicated hardware tinkering benches, and on-site guidance from academic and industry practitioners.
